Privacy

SWC Chess School · last updated 18 October 2026

The short version.

  • A parent (or an adult for themselves) signs in on the website and in the Android app. The account keeps the email address and the learners' progress, so it follows them from one device to another and allows games with friends and classmates.
  • There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics, and we never sell or share anyone's information.
  • One exception you choose yourself: on the Simran page, Watch live opens SGPC's live kirtan from Sachkhand Sri Harmandar Sahib in a YouTube player (youtube-nocookie.com). Nothing from YouTube loads until you press it, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that player.
  • Children are only ever shown to other players as an animal and a colour, never by name, and there is no chat.

Who we are

SWC Chess School is made by Shaheedi Bunga, a registered charity in England and Wales (number 1185103), 22 Beckett Road, Leicester LE5 0HE, as part of Sikh World Championships. Shaheedi Bunga is responsible for the personal information described here. Contact: gurmat@shaheedibunga.com.

On the device

The lessons, the chess computer (Stockfish), the puzzles and the opening book are all inside the app and the website, so they work without sending anything. Games played against the bots, Insights and the settings are kept on the device only. Neither the app nor the website uses cookies or anything else for tracking, advertising or analytics.

The account

To use the school, a parent, or an adult for themselves, signs in with an email link or with Google, on the website or in the Android app. Children under 13 do not create accounts themselves: the account belongs to the parent, who adds each child as a profile shown by an animal and a colour.

What we keepWhy
The account's email addressTo sign in. We never email marketing to it.
Each child's profile: an animal, a colour and an age group (young, junior or adult look)To show the right lessons and keep each child's progress separate. No names or dates of birth are asked for.
Learning progress: lessons, stars, puzzle results, ratings and streaksSo progress follows the learner from one device to another.
Games with friends or classmates: the moves, the result and the clock timesTo play the game and show it afterwards.
Friendships and class membershipsChildren can only play friends their parent has accepted, and classmates in a teacher's class.
In a class: each child's animal, colour and a summary of their progress, seen by that class's teacherSo the teacher can follow the class. Only teachers approved by Shaheedi Bunga can run a class.
For adults (18+) who choose rated play: an online ratingTo pair them with players of similar strength. Adults and children are never paired.

This information is stored by our database provider, Supabase, in London (UK). The legal basis is the consent of the parent or adult who signs in, and our legitimate interest in running the school safely. We do not sell, share or rent personal information to anyone. Sign-in with Google is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: Google gives us the email address, and may also pass the name and profile picture on the Google account. We do not use or show the name or picture anywhere, and they are deleted with the account.

Keeping children safe

  • Other players see a child only as an animal and a colour.
  • There is no chat and no messaging: only four fixed friendly messages (such as "Good game").
  • Children play only friends approved by their parent, or classmates in a teacher's class.
  • Adults and children never meet in online play.

Your rights and deleting your data

A signed-in parent or adult can delete the account and everything stored with it from Settings → Account → Delete my account in the app. You can also ask us to see, correct or delete your information by emailing gurmat@shaheedibunga.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Open source

The app's code is public at github.com/SchoolShaheedi/scc-chess-school, so anyone can check what it does.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. A change in what we collect will be shown in the app before it applies.
